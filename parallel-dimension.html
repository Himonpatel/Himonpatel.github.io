01 · PREMISE

A story first. A game possibility second.

The project originated from a dream and was later developed into a larger fictional concept. This page deliberately avoids publishing every plot detail; the focus is the process of turning an unstructured idea into a more coherent narrative world.

02 · WORLDBUILDING

The Hollow should feel familiar before it feels impossible.

THE HOLLOW

Wrong silence. Familiar streets. Organic cocoons. Predators that turn recognition into threat.

Familiar geography

The world becomes unsettling because recognizable spaces can appear where they should not.

Threat hierarchy

The two-headed hounds are visible danger, but the world is designed to imply that they are not the deepest mystery.

Cocoons

The cocoon imagery turns the environment itself into evidence that something biological and dimensional is happening.

Incomplete knowledge

The story does not explain the world immediately. Discovery is part of the tension.

03 · GAME POSSIBILITY

What would make the story feel interactive rather than merely illustrated?

The concept could eventually use exploration, player perspective, discoveries and choices to change what the player understands about the world. These are design directions, not claims that a working game has already been built.
