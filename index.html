How I work

I try to separate what I know from what I’m assuming.

01

Frame

Start with the user, context and actual problem before jumping to a feature.

02

Investigate

Use public evidence, conversations, reviews and product research while keeping limitations visible.

03

Build

Prototype enough of the system to expose technical assumptions and implementation constraints.

04

Test the claim

Separate technical validation from product, behavioural and ecosystem validation.
