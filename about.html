MY PATH

Why the portfolio looks different.

Most of the work in this portfolio was created over the past year. My direction sharpened when a small EdTech idea I submitted for a buildathon kept growing; it eventually became Project Atlas. Since then, I have concentrated on a smaller number of deeper projects rather than collecting tutorial applications.

I have also been trying to form teams around projects and hackathons since my third semester. Those attempts reinforced how much ambitious work depends on shared understanding, commitment and complementary skills, while also pushing me to become more technically independent.

The portfolio is intended to show both how I reason about problems and what I can currently build — without making claims that the evidence does not support.
