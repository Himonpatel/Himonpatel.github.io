Gamified Learning Game Jam

An academic initiative exploring how game systems, feedback and interaction can support learning beyond adding points, badges and streaks.

DISCUSSIONS ONGOING · COLLABORATION NOT FINALIZED

01 · MOTIVATION

Gamification is bigger than adding points.

The initiative came from seeing how easily gamified learning can be reduced to rewards layered over ordinary lessons. I wanted students to think about systems, motivation, progression, interaction and educational experience design more deeply.

The small interaction above is intentional

It now demonstrates the action → feedback → adaptation loop with a small interactive UI instead of leaving the concept as a static graphic.

02 · RESEARCH

The initiative was informed by broader EdTech questions.

India EdTech context

Research into major failures and the wider funding decline helped frame questions about engagement and real product value.

China after Double Reduction

Research into market changes and AI learning devices broadened the comparison beyond India.

03 · MY CONTRIBUTION

The plan changed, so the work changed with it.

I proposed the initiative within my college, prepared awareness-session material and explained the concept to faculty. I also helped connect the college with an external professional contact working in gamified learning.

04 · STAKEHOLDERS

External conversations without turning them into a partnership claim.

Discussions involving the college and Press Start Academy are still ongoing. I can accurately say that I proposed the initiative and helped connect stakeholders; I cannot claim a finalized collaboration.

05 · CURRENT STATUS

Still evolving.

The initiative is not a completed event. The work completed so far includes the proposal, research/material preparation, faculty conversations and external introduction.
